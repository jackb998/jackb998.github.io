How it started

“A mediocre athlete, but I love it, so I keep doing it.”

It all began in 2024, in a swimming pool with my flatmate. We went back to swimming to get fit again, with no big plans: one lane, a few lengths and the promise to show up the following week. Then the motivation grew, and with it the need for a goal. I added running and cycling, and set myself a simple rule: every race should be a little bigger than the last one.

Each challenge keeps me motivated and pushes me one step past what I thought I could do.

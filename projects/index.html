Rondelle

2026

File tools, thoughtfully arranged · app for macOS

A menu bar app for macOS that turns a file drag into a conversion: while dragging from the Finder, press ⇧ Shift and a wheel shows the formats for that kind of file. It works with images, PDFs, videos, audio, documents, spreadsheets and presentations. Everything runs on the Mac: nothing is uploaded.

Built in Swift on Apple frameworks, available in English, Italian and French. Free and open source (MIT licence).
